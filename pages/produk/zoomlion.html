Tentang Zoomlion

Zoomlion adalah produsen mesin konstruksi dan pertanian asal Changsha, Tiongkok, yang lini mesin pertaniannya kini dipakai di banyak negara. Untuk sektor pertanian, Zoomlion memproduksi rangkaian lengkap dari pengolahan tanah hingga pascapanen: traktor, mesin tanam padi, combine harvester, mesin panen tebu, mesin pengering gabah, baler, dan implement traktor.

Sebagai distributor resmi Zoomlion di Jakarta, PT Diesel Agri Sukses Sejahtera (DASS) menyediakan unit untuk petani, kelompok tani, perkebunan, penggilingan, dan perusahaan agribisnis di seluruh Indonesia. About Zoomlion

Zoomlion is a construction and agricultural machinery manufacturer based in Changsha, China, whose agricultural range is now used in many countries. For farming, Zoomlion builds a complete line from land preparation to post-harvest: tractors, rice transplanters, combine harvesters, sugarcane harvesters, grain dryers, balers, and tractor implements.

As the authorised Zoomlion distributor in Jakarta, PT Diesel Agri Sukses Sejahtera (DASS) supplies units to farmers, farmer groups, plantations, rice mills, and agribusinesses across Indonesia. Every product page lists full specifications so you can compare units before contacting our team.

Zoomlion Product Range

  • Tractors from 50 HP up to high-horsepower classes, including hybrid models, for paddy fields, dry land, and plantations.
  • Combine harvesters for rice, wheat, corn, and soybeans, from compact to high-capacity classes.
  • Sugarcane harvesters, rice transplanters, and balers to mechanise planting through harvest.
  • Grain dryers from 10 to 30 tonnes per batch to protect harvest quality.
