Brand Profile

About VectorAgr

VectorAgr brings precision agriculture technology together in one ecosystem: agricultural drones, tractor auto steering systems, autonomous farm robots, and a digital agriculture app. These devices connect with each other, so field data and work plans flow from the app to machines in the field.

VectorAgr solutions suit plantations, agribusinesses, and modern farmers who want to lower input costs, reduce reliance on labour, and work more precisely. Through DASS, you can choose the devices that match the scale and type of your land.

VectorAgr Product Range

  • V80, V180, HD525, and HD540S agricultural drones for spraying and spreading.
  • HD408, HD812, and HD818 auto steering for straight, precise tractor passes.
  • VH300 robot for autonomous spraying and hauling in orchards and greenhouses.
  • HD Digital Ag Solution for zone maps, NDVI maps, and variable rate application (VRA).
