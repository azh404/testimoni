Produk apa saja yang dijual DASS?

Kami adalah distributor resmi Zoomlion (traktor, traktor hybrid, combine harvester, mesin panen tebu, mesin tanam padi, mesin pengering gabah, mesin baler, dan implement), EAVision (drone sprayer pertanian), serta VectorAgr (drone pertanian, auto steering traktor, dan robot pertanian). Lihat semua di menu Produk.

Di mana lokasi kantor DASS?

Kantor kami berada di Jalan River Garden Boulevard No. 19B Blok B2, Cakung Timur, Jakarta Timur. Jam operasional Senin–Jumat, 08.00–17.00 WIB. Lihat peta di halaman Kontak.

Bagaimana cara mengetahui harga unit?

Harga bergantung pada model, konfigurasi, dan kebutuhan Anda. Klik tombol hijau WhatsApp di pojok kanan bawah lalu pilih Tanya harga & ketersediaan, tim kami akan mengirimkan penawaran.

Bagaimana memilih unit yang tepat untuk lahan saya?

Gunakan Kalkulator Pilih Traktor di setiap halaman produk Zoomlion dan kalkulator semprot di setiap halaman drone, atau bandingkan spesifikasi di halaman Bandingkan Produk. Tim kami juga siap membantu lewat WhatsApp.

Apakah tersedia brosur produk?

Ya. Setiap halaman produk memiliki tombol Download PDF berisi foto, keunggulan, dan spesifikasi. Anda juga bisa meminta brosur lewat WhatsApp.

Apa perbedaan drone EAVision dan VectorAgr?

Keduanya drone pertanian untuk penyemprotan. EAVision berfokus pada drone sprayer (seri J dan EA) dalam berbagai kapasitas tangki, sedangkan VectorAgr juga menyediakan auto steering traktor dan robot pertanian. Bandingkan spesifikasinya di halaman Bandingkan Produk.

Apakah unit bisa dikirim ke luar Jakarta?

Silakan hubungi tim kami lewat WhatsApp dengan menyebutkan lokasi Anda untuk informasi pengiriman dan ketersediaan unit.

Kapan pesan WhatsApp saya dibalas?

Pesan dibalas pada jam kerja, Senin–Jumat 08.00–17.00 WIB. Pesan di luar jam kerja akan dibalas pada jam kerja berikutnya.

What products does DASS sell?

We are the authorised distributor of Zoomlion (tractors, hybrid tractors, combine harvesters, sugarcane harvesters, rice transplanters, grain dryers, balers, and implements), EAVision (agricultural sprayer drones), and VectorAgr (agricultural drones, tractor auto steering, and agricultural robots). See them all under Products.

Where is the DASS office?

Our office is at Jalan River Garden Boulevard No. 19B Blok B2, Cakung Timur, Jakarta Timur. Opening hours: Monday–Friday, 08:00–17:00 WIB. See the map on the Contact page.

How can I find out the price of a unit?

Prices depend on the model, configuration, and your needs. Tap the green WhatsApp button at the bottom right and choose Ask about price & availability; our team will send you a quotation.

How do I choose the right unit for my land?

Use the Tractor Selection Calculator on every Zoomlion product page and the spraying calculator on every drone page, or compare specifications on the Compare Products page. Our team can also help on WhatsApp.

Are product brochures available?

Yes. Every product page has a Download PDF button with photos, key features, and specifications. You can also request a brochure on WhatsApp.

What is the difference between EAVision and VectorAgr drones?

Both are agricultural spraying drones. EAVision focuses on sprayer drones (J and EA series) in various tank capacities, while VectorAgr also offers tractor auto steering and agricultural robots. Compare specifications on the Compare Products page.

Can units be delivered outside Jakarta?

Please contact our team on WhatsApp with your location for delivery and availability information.

When will my WhatsApp message be answered?

Messages are answered during working hours, Monday–Friday 08:00–17:00 WIB. Messages sent outside working hours are answered during the next working hours.
