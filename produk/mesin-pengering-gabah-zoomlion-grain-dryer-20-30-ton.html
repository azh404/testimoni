Buying Guide

Who Is the Zoomlion Grain Dryer 20–30 Ton For?

The Zoomlion Grain Dryer 20–30 Ton is designed for rice mills and grain processing centres that receive large volumes at peak harvest. With an effective volume of 36.77–53.9 m³ per batch, grain from many farmers can be dried quickly before its quality drops.

A low-speed screw conveyor and low-breakage elevator keep kernels intact for better milling yield. Heat sources are flexible: biomass, rice husk, or oil or gas burners, matched to the fuel available on site.
